Help build our new Social Hall.

Since 1981, Saint Mary's has been a home for Romanian Orthodox families in North Texas. Our community has outgrown the hall that served it. Your gift helps build a new Social Hall & Cultural Center, a place worthy of our faith, our heritage and of Colleyville.
