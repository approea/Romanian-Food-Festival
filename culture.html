03 / Culture

Music. Dance. Tradition. Community.

Romanian culture is vibrant, layered and deeply rooted in tradition. Here it is presented with restraint.

Dance ensemble

Alunelul Dance Group

Alunelul was created to promote Romanian culture and preserve it in the minds and hearts of younger Romanian generations in the United States. Alunelul—one of the first traditional dances Romanian children learn—lends the group its name.

Dancers grow up within the group, moving from its youngest members into Colleyville's older Romanian folk dance ensembles as they mature.

— Oana Lepadatu

Dance ensemble

Tricolorii Veseli Juniori

Tricolorii Veseli Juniori is a Romanian folk dance ensemble for children and young people roughly ages 9 to 18, continuing the tradition established by the community's adult Romanian dancers.

The group performs regional dances from across Romania and has taken part in the Romanian Food Festival for many years running.

— Stefan Mitranca

The visual language

One motif.
Used well.

The folk emblem appears only at key moments: loading, section openings and background watermarks.

The burgundy palette replaces generic flag colors and keeps the experience contemporary.
