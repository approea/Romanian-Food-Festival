Romanian Food Festival DFW

The family of Saint Mary's Romanian Orthodox Church, parishioners and community members warmly welcome guests to celebrate Romanian culture in Colleyville, Texas.

Our story

Romania, remembered through the table, the song and the circle.

The Romanian people have a rich and ancient culture reflected in Orthodox faith, food, music, dance, tradition and community life.

The festival exists to preserve these traditions while sharing them with friends and neighbors throughout North Texas—generous food, live tradition, a welcoming atmosphere and time spent together.

This site is intentionally editorial and simple—the same design philosophy that shapes the festival itself.

About the festival

Rooted since 1981.

Saint Mary's Romanian Orthodox Church represents one of the earliest Romanian Orthodox communities in Texas, founded in 1981. The parish is part of the Romanian Orthodox Episcopate of America.

The Romanian Food Festival DFW is organized each year by the parishioners and families of Saint Mary's, bringing the community's food, faith and folk traditions to friends and neighbors across North Texas.
