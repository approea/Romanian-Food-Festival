The Festival

A refined celebration of Romanian heritage in Dallas—built around food, music, tradition and the warmth of gathering together.

Our story

Romania, remembered through the table, the song and the circle.

The Romanian Food Festival brings together generations of families, friends and guests who want to experience Romanian hospitality in a genuine way.

Rather than overloading the experience, the festival focuses on the things that matter: generous food, live tradition, a welcoming atmosphere and time spent together.

This site is intentionally editorial and simple—the same design philosophy that shapes the festival itself.
